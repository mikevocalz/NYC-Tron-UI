## Roboto advance widths (data only)

`dist/fonts/roboto-static-regular.json` holds the horizontal advance width of
each Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A and -B and General Punctuation
character in `RobotoStatic-Regular.ttf` version 2.138, read from
`/system/fonts/` in the Android 16 (API 36) emulator system image. No glyph
outlines, kerning or other font data are included. The launcher label check
uses the widths to measure Android Launcher3 labels without the font.
